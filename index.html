The evening

Golf with friends, after dark

This is the first ever ESCP Golf Society event, and we wanted it to feel like a night out. A fun evening with good people, a bit of sport and a well-stocked bar.

Step into a bay and play almost any course in the world, take on the challenges against your friends, or grab a drink and watch from the sofa. Whether you play every weekend or have never held a club, you'll have a good time.

Any course in the world

The simulators let you play famous courses from almost anywhere, shot by shot.

Challenges

Compete against each other in simulator challenges and see who comes out on top.

Drinks at the bar

Cold soft drinks, beers and cocktails, plus coffee if you need it.

Clubs provided

Club bags are ready in every bay, so you don't need to bring anything.

Contact & policies

Good to know

Contact

Questions about the event or your ticket? Email the ESCP Golf Society at antonius.mueller@edu.escp.eu and we'll get back to you within two days.

Refunds & cancellations

Tickets are non-refundable. If you can't make it, you're welcome to pass your ticket on to a friend; just email us their name. If we cancel or reschedule the event, you'll get a full refund.

Alcohol & ID

Alcohol is only served to guests aged 18 and over. Bring a valid photo ID, such as a passport or driving licence, or the bar can't serve you alcohol.
